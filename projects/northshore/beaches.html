Overview

The North Shore is known for sandy beaches, clear water, and big waves. This page highlights a few famous spots and what each one is known for.

Sunset Beach

Sunset Beach has a long shoreline and great views.

Banzai Pipeline (Ehukai)

Pipeline is one of the most famous surf breaks in the world.

Waimea Bay

Waimea Bay is a wide, beautiful beach with a deep blue bay.
