Goku's Family

Goku, the main protagonist of the Dragon Ball series, has a family that includes his wife and children. Here is a brief overview of Goku's family:

Chi-Chi

Chi-Chi is Goku's wife. She is a strong-willed and protective woman who is deeply devoted to her family.

Gohan

Gohan is Goku's eldest son. He is a talented martial artist and a kind-hearted individual.

Goten

Goten is Goku's younger son. He is playful and energetic, with a natural talent for martial arts.

Goku's Family Fast Facts

  • Goku's daily grocery bill is over 100,000 Zeni.
  • Chi-Chi is officially the scariest entity in Universe 7.
  • Their closest neighbor is a giant dinosaur.
